This is the final project for [STA303:Artificial Intelligence](https://fangkongx.github.io/Teaching/STA303/Fall2025/index.html). We compared a range of RL algorithms, using CartPole-v1 as a common testbed.

- Online: *DQN*, *PPO*, *A2C*, and *Rainbow*.
- Offline: *BC*, *BCQ*, and a simplified *World Model*, each trying to replicate or improve on an expert policy.

The full methodology and results are in the [project report (PDF)](/blog/comparing-rl-methods-on-cartpole/report.pdf).
